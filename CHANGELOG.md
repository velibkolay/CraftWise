# Changelog

Release notes per version; the release workflow uses the section matching the tag (or "Unreleased").

## Unreleased
- Profit table for every recipe you know or can learn: reagent cost (vendor or Auctionator), sale value after the AH cut, profit.
- Learn view: recipes you don't know yet, the skill they need, trainer fee and where to get them.
- Trainer fees and vendor prices recorded in game; learned recipes leave the Learn view right away.
- Bags view: vendor or auction for every item you carry; keep items out of the advice.
- Upgrades view: craftable items that beat what you wear (item level, armour), with dismiss/restore.
- Skill-up log: every craft recorded with your skill and whether it gave a point; yellow/grey thresholds in tooltips.
- Music tab: a song per profession while you cast it (resume or start over, volume), optional video window, and a Party key (dance + song + video).
